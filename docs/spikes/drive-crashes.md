## 1. `hospital-road-ne-back`, (141, 276): front-left wheel into a 0.40 m kerb

- **Line.** The car drives west at 13.2 m/s with no braking, on `dr_Hospital_Road_12_s1_b`. That road is a one-way slip, 3.4 m wide, with one lane. The offset shrinks slowly along the single 51.9 m graph edge from `HR26_ai2` (3 lanes `+++`, radius 3.19) to `HR12_b2` (1 lane, radius 1.7). That fits the AI still holding the left lane of the 3-lane approach after the fork.
- **Kerb.** The sidewalk Colmesh (`sidewalks_0_1.dae`) has a vertical face 0.37 to 0.40 m tall (0.35 to 0.44 m above the road decal) at the slip's south edge, 1.68 m from its centre, from x 131 to 136, flaring out to x 148. Across the whole slice, the median face is 0.25 m and the 75th percentile is 0.40 m, so a quarter of all faces are this tall.
- **Contact.** The car's outline first overlaps kerb triangles (top +0.38 m) at t 7.45. At t 7.53 to 7.58 the lateral velocity jumps from -2.26 to -0.17 m/s and the heading turns 260° -> 271°, a push on the front-left. Damage registers in the batch from 7.40 (4.88 to 4.98 million). Terrain under the wheels is smooth (no step over 0.05 m), and the nearest building is 42 m away. The one-way signs are 0.6 m clear of the car's side, with their plates above roof height.
- **Game check.** `crash_damage`: part `covet_wheeldata_F` "Front Spindles" score 1.0, zone FL 0.048, no tyre down, `beamstate.damage` 12.8.
- **Verdict.** The front-left wheel and spindle struck a sidewalk kerb 0.40 m tall, which is higher than the 0.29 m tyre radius. The AI had run its left wheels about 0.9 m off the asphalt of a single-lane slip just after a 3-lane fork. Both defects are in the generated level: the kerb height (MapNG) and the lane count and graph at the fork (MapNG export or the BeamNG AI).

Speed and yaw stay smooth through the batch, with no impulse.
- **No object.** Within reach there is no static, building or sidewalk: the nearest kerb is 1.9 m from the car's outline, the give-way sign 4.8 m and building walls 9.9 m. No water, prefab, forest item or Road Architect session exists, and the in-lane step findings list nothing within 40 m.
- **Game check.** `crash_damage`: no damaged part, every zone 0, no tyre down, `beamstate.damage` 0.1 (dissipated energy). The `damageSum` rise therefore comes with no beam deformed or broken. The game notes that this section-damage API also counts scratches (`lua/ge/extensions/gameplay/util/crashDetection.lua:284`).
- **Terrain.** In t413, Lady Ishbel Avenue sits at 39.1 m and falls 3.1 m in about 24 m into Hospital Road, at 14.6% from x -212.6 to -194.6 (pitch 8.5°), then eases to 6 to 9%. The car passes a crest at x -211 (vertical acceleration -5.3) and then a sag at the junction mouth (+5.5 to +5.9 m/s², braking dive). Under the front overhang the terrain rises 0.10 m above the wheel plane. The front lip and bumper (jbeam z 0.18 to 0.20) ride at ref-node height, about 0.15 m above the terrain. The source is the GPXZ DEM: t413 is `flipud(gpxz.f32) - 8.88` within ±0.1 m, and the raw DEM drops 3.3 m in 12 m (27%) at x -203 to -191. MapNG's grading spread that drop out. In t403, built on the 30 m DEM, the junction was flat (36.2 to 36.1 m). The clockwise loop climbs the same ramp at 5.6 m/s with no braking, peaks at +2.8 m/s², and records no damage.
- **Verdict.** This is not a collision. Section damage fires with zero structural damage in the sag at the foot of a 14.6% ramp, while the car brakes and turns. The most likely mechanism is the low front scraping the terrain. That mechanism is inferred from the geometry and accelerations, not measured. The ramp comes from a 27% step in the GPXZ DEM, and whether the real street has it is unverified.
